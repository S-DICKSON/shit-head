Implement three mobile UX safety and visibility improvements: (1) double-tap confirmation on pickup pile to prevent accidental pickups, (2) compact timer repositioned to bottom-right corner as fixed overlay, (3) semi-transparent 8-card rendering on discard pile.

Purpose: These three features address the most impactful mobile playtesting feedback — accidental pile pickups ruin games, the timer takes up too much vertical space, and players cannot see the effective card when an 8 is on top of the pile.
Output: Updated PlayerCards, TurnTimer, PlayingPhase, and DiscardPile components plus new useDoubleTap composable.
</objective>
