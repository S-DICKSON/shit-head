**Changes to GameEngine.playCards():**

1. **Replace canPlayOn with canPlayOnPile** for card validation:
   - Import `canPlayOnPile` and `detectBurn` from `./CardRules`
   - Instead of checking `canPlayOn(playedCard, topCard)`, use `canPlayOnPile(playedCard, state.discardPile)`
   - This automatically handles: 2 always playable, 10 always playable, 8 always playable, 7-constraint, empty pile

2. **Detect burn after adding cards to pile:**
   - After cards are added to discardPile, call `detectBurn(newDiscardPile)`
   - If burn detected: clear discardPile to empty array, do NOT advance turn (same player goes again)
   - If no burn: advance turn normally (currentPlayerIndex + 1) % players.length

3. **Handle "cannot win on a 10" rule:**
   - After playing cards and before finalizing state:
   - If the played card is a 10 AND the player's hand would be empty AND drawPile is empty AND faceUp is empty AND faceDown is empty (i.e., this would be their last card): the 10 still plays, pile still burns, and player still gets another turn on empty pile. They must then play another card (or pick up if unable). The key is: the play is NOT rejected. The player simply cannot "exit" the game on a 10 because they get another turn.
   - Implementation: No special rejection needed. The natural flow handles this: 10 burns pile, same player goes again, player has empty hand -> Phase 7 will handle face-up/face-down progression. For Phase 6, just ensure 10 burn gives same player another turn.

4. **Handle four-of-a-kind burn mid-play:**
   - Same as 10 burn: clear pile, same player goes again
   - Burn detection happens after ALL played cards are added to pile (multi-card plays matter for four-of-a-kind)

**Updated playCards flow:**
```
1. Validate: phase, player, turn, indices, same-rank
2. Validate: canPlayOnPile(firstPlayedCard, state.discardPile)
3. Remove played cards from hand
4. Add played cards to discardPile
5. Auto-draw (if hand < 3 and drawPile has cards)
6. Check detectBurn(newDiscardPile):
   a. If burn: clear discardPile, keep currentPlayerIndex (same player)
   b. If no burn: advance currentPlayerIndex
7. Return new state
```
