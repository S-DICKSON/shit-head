Privacy Policy

This privacy policy describes how Karma by Splat Monkey handles information when you play the game.

Information We Collect

The information collected depends on how you access the game:

  • Discord Activity: When playing via Discord Activity, we receive your Discord user ID, display name, and avatar hash from the Discord SDK. This information is provided by Discord as part of the Activity authentication flow.
  • Web (Guest): When playing via the web browser without Discord, we only assign you a randomly generated display name (e.g., "Guest 1234"). No identifying information is collected.

We do not collect email addresses, passwords, or any other personal information.

How We Use Your Information

The information we receive is used exclusively to:

  • Display your name and avatar to other players in the game room
  • Manage game sessions and room membership

We do not use your information for analytics, tracking, or advertising of any kind.

Data Storage and Retention

  • All game data is stored in server memory only — nothing is written to disk or a database
  • Discord access tokens are held in server memory only and are never persisted to any storage
  • No cookies are used
  • All data is automatically deleted when the game session ends or the server restarts

Third-Party Services

Discord: If you play via Discord Activity, Discord's own privacy practices apply to the information they share with us. Please review Discord's Privacy Policy for details.

We use no other third-party services, analytics platforms, or tracking tools.

Children's Privacy

This service is not directed at children under the age of 13. When playing via Discord Activity, Discord's own age requirements and policies apply.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date at the bottom of this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please reach out on our Discord server.
